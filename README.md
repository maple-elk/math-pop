# 🎈 Math Pop! - Elementary Math, Brain Teasers & Science Lab

A fun, interactive, distraction-free educational web application designed for elementary students (Grades 2–5). Features single-key **Spacebar** progression to test mental math speed, solve curated spatial/logic riddles, and explore intuitive physics, engineering, and chemistry principles.

🌐 **Live Demo on GitHub Pages**: [https://maple-elk.github.io/math-pop/](https://maple-elk.github.io/math-pop/)

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (Local)

Run Math Pop! in two easy ways locally:

### Option 1: Double-Click or Open Directly (Instant)
Simply open `index.html` in any web browser:
```bash
open index.html
```

### Option 2: Local Server
```bash
python3 server.py
```
Serves the app locally and opens your default browser.

## ⌨️ How to Play (Keyboard-First UX)

Designed for rapid, frictionless learning:
1. **Challenge Appears**: A randomized math problem or numbered teaser/science dilemma appears.
2. **Press [SPACE BAR]**: Reveals the solution, visual counters/diagrams, and an intuitive explanation.
3. **Press [SPACE BAR] Again**: Advances immediately to the next problem or slide!

*Optional Input: Children can also type their answer in the text box before hitting Space to test their typing and accuracy, or use the mouse/touchscreen to tap the big button or navigation arrows.*

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🌱 Kindergarten Level (48 Curated Numbered Slides)
- **🔢 Counting & Math (12 Slides)**: Counting ducklings, apple tree addition ($3 + 2 = 5$), balloon pop subtraction ($5 - 2 = 3$), ladybug ten-frames, comparing quantities (Which fishbowl has more?), night stars, cookie jar additions, frog jumps, high-five fingers, toy racecar addition ($2 + 2 = 4$), triangle sides, and making 10.
- **❓ Curious Questions (12 Slides)**: Primary color mixing (Blue + Yellow = Green), 8 spider legs, soaring eagles vs. land animals, winter snow, 5 senses (smelling roses with the nose), egg-laying birds, caterpillar metamorphosis into a butterfly, melting ice cubes, the daytime sun, banana peeling, 7 days of the week, and water + sunshine for plant growth.
- **🦁 Playful Riddles (12 Slides)**: Whimsical rhyming & "Who Am I?" riddles with joyful SVG artwork (Ticking clock, purring kitten, crack-open egg, buzzing honeybee, rain umbrella, slow snail, winter snowman, wagging puppy, birthday candle, mighty elephant, glowing night moon, and pond frog).
- **🧩 Shape & Logic Puzzles (12 Slides)**: Repeating color patterns (Red/Blue), shape sequences (Triangle/Square), odd-one-out categories (Fruit vs. Animals, Vehicles vs. Lion), butterfly silhouette shadow matching, size ordering (Smallest to Biggest), full vs. empty juice cups, matching pairs (Shoe & Sock), missing puzzle pieces, and animal homes (Honeybee in Beehive).
- **🔊 Read-to-Me Voice**: Native browser text-to-speech button (`🔊 Listen`) so young learners and pre-readers can hear questions and explanations read aloud.

### 2. 📚 Elementary Math (Grades 2 – 5)
- **2nd Grade**: Addition (to 20), Subtraction (to 20), Mixed mode, Challenge (to 100), plus visual ten-frame dot counters.
- **3rd Grade**: Times Tables (up to $10 \times 10$), Division Facts, 3-Digit Addition & Subtraction, Perimeter & Area basics.
- **4th Grade**: Multi-Digit Multiplication, Long Division Facts, Mixed Operations, Fraction Equivalence.
- **5th Grade**: Order of Operations (PEMDAS), Decimals, Fraction Addition/Multiplication, Coordinate Plane fundamentals.
- **🦄 Unusual Math**: Surprising giant numbers ($1000 + 1000$, $100 \times 100 = 10,000$), zero black holes ($7 \times 8 \times 0 = 0$), zero exponents ($999^0 = 1$), and below-zero numbers ($5 - 10 = -5$).

### 3. 🧩 Brain Teasers & Riddles (28 Curated Numbered Slides)
- **🕵️ Logic Riddles (12 Slides)**: Lateral thinking puzzles and wordplay (Odd Seven, 2 Apples Trap, 2nd Place Runner, Hens & Eggs rate, Guess My Number, Feathers vs. Rocks, 3-Person Fishing Family, Zero Phone Keypad, 12-inch Nose, Hopscotch Multiples, Age Riddle, and Carton of Eggs).
- **🎨 Visual & Spatial (10 Slides)**: Shape sequences, 3D isometric cube stacks with hidden blocks, butterfly symmetry, triangle detective, rotating arrows, 3D staircases, heart mirror line, top-down orthographic grids, paper fold & hole punches, and cube nets.
- **🧠 Mental 3D (6 Slides)**: Mental visualization of 3D solids revealed on Spacebar (Cube straight edges, pyramid vertices, cylinder faces, cone cross sections, prism vertices, and shoebox faces).

### 4. 🔬 Science Lab: Physics & Engineering (16 Curated Numbered Slides)
- **🌌 Space & Gravity (4 Slides)**: Apollo 15 hammer & feather vacuum acceleration, sound in space (wave transmission), ISS perpetual free-fall orbit, and Moon vs. Earth gravity scales.
- **💨 Air Resistance & Friction (4 Slides)**: Flat paper vs. crumpled ball surface drag, parachute canopy air traps, sledding on ice vs. grass, and rolling vs. sliding friction with ball bearings.
- **🌊 Density & Viscosity (4 Slides)**: Giant steel ship vs. tiny pebble (Archimedes' Principle), oil and water density tower, honey vs. water ramp race (internal fluid friction), and the floating ice hexagon crystal anomaly.
- **⚗️ Chemistry & Matter (4 Slides)**: Baking soda & vinegar balloon gas expansion ($CO_2$), playground seesaw lever mechanics ($\text{Torque} = \text{Force} \times \text{Distance}$), candle under a jar atmospheric pressure, and dissolving sugar molecules.

## 🛠️ Technology & Architecture

- **Zero Dependencies**: Pure HTML5, CSS3, and modern vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, build steps, bundlers, or npm packages required.
- **Web Audio API**: Built-in sound synthesis for chimes and pops with a mute toggle.
- **HTML5 Canvas**: Lightweight particle confetti celebration system on 5-star streaks.
- **Local Storage**: Automatically persists streaks and completed counters locally.
- **Responsive & Accessible**: Works seamlessly on desktops, Chromebooks, iPads, and smartphones.
